## Scope and mode
The brew-day screen of the CraftBeerPi web UI: the home screen the brewer runs a brew from on his electric 3-vessel HERMS. Visitor mode: Operate.

## Audience, job and content
- The brewer at the rig in his garage: monitor first (mouse and keyboard), phone at times, often with wet hands, often glancing from across the room.
- Job: see what is heating and how close each vessel is to its target; follow the recipe step now and next; switch elements, pumps, AUTO/MANUAL and targets; answer hop and lid alerts.
- Content: live PV/SV per vessel, element output, mode, pump state; the recipe's steps, timers and additions; the answer to every command.
- Constraints: proposal only (nothing changed in craftbeerpi4-ui, craftbeerpi4 or cbpi-testbench); fermentation out of scope; recording the brew day belongs to BrewScribe.

## Chosen direction: layout B (the brewer's choice, 9 Nov 2026, from a working A/B comparison)
- Recipe column on the left. Three PID faceplates across the top: big red PV over smaller green SV, OUT with a bar, MODE. A line drawing of the rig below, each vessel under its own faceplate. Alarms in the title bar; last event and screen tabs under it.
- Phone: current step with the next one, faceplates stacked, recipe below, two rows of tabs at the bottom; no drawing.
- Memorable moment: from across the garage, three black PID windows with red digits over green, lamp strips lit orange and green, and gold wort moving through the coil inside the hot liquor tank.
- Approved comp: .impeccable/mocks/layout-d3-faceplates-recipe-left.png. Built mockup: proposal/brew-day.html (B is the default; ?layout=a shows the alternative). A/B record: proposal/compare.html. What each change needs: proposal/BUILD-NOTES.md.

## Accepted deviations from the comp
- Phone density: the first phone screen shows about one and a third vessels, against about two and a half in the comp. The comp gets there with buttons about 36 px tall; this build keeps every control at 44 px or more (lamp buttons 56, mode row 50, tab rows 48) for wet hands (defect U2, product principle 4: the controls do not shrink).
- The current step shows its time left (23:41 LEFT) where the comp shows a play marker; steps still to come keep the comp's ring.
